**✅ Llama Mascot Brand Bible**

### **Official Mascot Name**
**“Llamy”** (or “Llama” / “Profesor Llama” – you can choose)

---

### **Core Visual Style**

**Art Style**:  
- Clean, bold vector cartoon style  
- Thick, consistent dark blue outlines  
- Flat colors with minimal shading for a friendly, approachable look  
- Kid-friendly, warm, and energetic  
- High contrast for excellent visibility on websites, apps, and printed merchandise

**Color Palette**:
- **Llama Wool**: Pure white (#FFFFFF) with very subtle light blue shading (#E8F0FF) for depth
- **Outlines**: Deep blue (#1E3A8A or similar rich navy)
- **Bandana**: Bright red (#EF4444) with darker red accents for folds
- **Eyes**: Dark blue/black pupils with white sparkle highlights
- **Inner Ears**: Soft pink (#F9A8D4)
- **Nose/Mouth**: Soft pink
- **Accessories** (backpack, headphones, pencils, books): Bright, saturated colors that complement the red bandana

---

### **Character Design Guidelines**

**Head & Face**:
- Large, rounded head (slightly oversized for cuteness)
- Big, expressive eyes — always friendly and engaged
- Consistent ear shape: Rounded with pink interiors
- Small, rounded snout with pink nose
- Warm, smiling mouth (open or closed smile depending on activity)

**Body**:
- Fluffy, rounded wool texture suggested through simple curved lines
- Small, cute limbs with dark blue/black hooves
- Red bandana tied around the neck — **always present** as signature element

**Proportions**:
- Big head + smaller body = maximum cuteness
- Consistent height-to-width ratio across poses

### **Do’s and Don’ts**

**Do**:
- Always use bold blue outlines
- Keep the red bandana as a consistent signature
- Maintain friendly, enthusiastic expressions
- Use the same eye style across all versions
- Keep background transparent for versatility

**Don’t**:
- Change the bandana color
- Use thin or sketchy lines
- Make the eyes too small or angry
- Add complex patterns or textures to the wool
- Use realistic shading or 3D effects

---

### **Usage Guidelines**

**Website / Digital**:
- Transparent PNG files
- Multiple sizes (small icons, medium illustrations, large hero images)

**Merchandise**:
- Simplified versions for printing (slightly thicker lines, fewer small details)
- Works well on white, red, or navy apparel
- Excellent for stickers, plush toys, tote bags, etc.

**Color Variations**:
- Full color (primary)
- Monochrome blue (for single-color printing)
- White outline version (for dark backgrounds)
