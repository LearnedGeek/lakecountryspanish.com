### **Style & Proportion Guidelines**

**Key Characteristics**:
- **Line Weight**: Bold, consistent dark blue outlines (never thin or sketchy)
- **Head-to-Body Ratio**: Large head (about 45-50% of total height) for maximum cuteness
- **Eyes**: Large, round, and expressive — always positioned symmetrically with highlights
- **Bandana**: Always present, tied on the left side with visible knot
- **Pose Style**: Dynamic but simple — clear silhouettes for easy recognition at small sizes
- **Overall Vibe**: Enthusiastic, friendly, trustworthy, and fun for kids and parents

**Do’s**:
- Always maintain the red bandana
- Use consistent bold blue outlines
- Keep expressions positive and energetic
- Use transparent backgrounds for digital assets

**Don’ts**:
- Change the bandana color
- Use gradients or heavy shading
- Alter the eye style or head shape
- Add extra accessories unless approved for specific poses

### **Recommended Usage**

**Digital**:
- Hero images, icons, buttons, loading animations
- Pair with Spanish text (e.g., “¡Hola!”, “¡Muy bien!”, “Vamos a aprender”)

**Merchandise**:
- T-shirts, hoodies, stickers, plush toys
- Works excellently in full color and single-color (blue or red) versions

**File Deliverables You Should Have**:
- Master Reference (this front-facing version)
- Transparent PNGs in multiple sizes
- Simplified versions for printing
- Black & white / single color variants
