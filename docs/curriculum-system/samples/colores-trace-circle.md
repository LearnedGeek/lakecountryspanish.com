:::lesson-overview
**Worksheet:** Traza y Colorea — *Trace and Color*
**For:** K4–2nd grade
**Time:** 4–6 minutes
**Goal:** Practice color word recognition by tracing the Spanish word and coloring the matching picture.
:::

:::teacher-note
Each student gets one worksheet. Pencils or fine markers for tracing; crayons for coloring. Heritage speakers may already know these — encourage them to teach a friend nearby. Early finishers can flip the sheet and draw something else from the room in that color.
:::

**Instructions for students:** *Traza la palabra. Después colorea el dibujo del mismo color.*
