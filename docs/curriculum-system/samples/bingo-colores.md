:::lesson-overview
**Game:** Bingo de Colores
**For:** K4–2nd grade
**Plays:** 5–8 minutes
**Goal:** Reinforce the six color words by listening and matching.
:::

:::teacher-note
Print one card per student. Optionally print on cardstock for reuse across the year. The 🦙 free spaces are intentional — they give younger kids an easy win and keep the pace lively. After Bingo is called, encourage the winner to **say each color in Spanish** as they show their winning row.
:::

:::bingo-card
| 🔴 rojo | 🔵 azul | 🟡 amarillo |
|---|---|---|
| 🦙 | 🟢 verde | 🦙 |
| 🟠 anaranjado | 🟣 morado | 🦙 |
:::
