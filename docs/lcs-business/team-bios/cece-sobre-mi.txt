Born and raised in Lima, Peru, Cece Kemnitzer has called the United
States home for the past 25 years. She lives in Pewaukee, Wisconsin,
with her husband and their 9-year-old twin boys.

For more than 15 years, Cece has dedicated her career to teaching
Spanish to children and adults. Her greatest passion is helping young
learners discover that learning a second language can be exciting,
meaningful, and fun.

After years of teaching in classrooms, Cece realized that many
language programs focus heavily on memorizing grammar and vocabulary.
She believed there was a better way. Inspired by her students, she
began designing her own curriculum with one goal in mind: helping
students communicate confidently in Spanish.

As the co-founder of Lake Country Spanish, Cece has created a unique
curriculum that combines conversation, movement, games, music,
culture, and meaningful repetition so students naturally build
confidence speaking Spanish in everyday situations.

Cece believes that learning a language is about much more than grammar
and vocabulary. It is an opportunity to explore the people,
traditions, music, geography, history, and everyday life of the
Spanish-speaking world.

She is passionate about helping children understand that the
Spanish-speaking world is made up of more than twenty unique
countries, each with its own customs, traditions, cuisine, music, and
history. Through cultural understanding, students develop curiosity,
empathy, and respect for people whose lives and experiences may be
different from their own.

Before moving to Wisconsin, Cece owned and operated her own business
in the Chicago area, where she developed valuable experience as an
entrepreneur and educator. She studied at Universidad del Pacífico in
Lima, Peru, and proudly shares her Peruvian heritage in every class
she teaches.

Cece believes that every child can learn a second language when they
feel confident, supported, and inspired. Her mission is not only to
teach Spanish but also to help students become culturally aware,
globally minded, and respectful citizens of an increasingly connected
world.
